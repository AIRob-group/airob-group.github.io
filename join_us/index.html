Opportunities

The SFU AIRob Lab is always looking for self-motivated students at all levels. If you are interested in working with us on AI, robotics, and multi-agent/robot systems, please mention lab director Hang Ma in your application to the SFU CS graduate program.

Applicants should also refer to the SFU CS graduate program page for more information on the admission requirements and application deadlines . Eligible students with only a bachelor’s degree are encouraged to apply directly to the PhD program.

Location

The SFU Burnaby campus is located on Burnaby Mountain, about 12 miles from downtown Vancouver, offering easy access to a vibrant metropolitan area while being surrounded by nature.
